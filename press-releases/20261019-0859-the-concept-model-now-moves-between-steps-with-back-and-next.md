# The Concept Model Now Moves Between Steps With Back and Next

**19 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released a Back and Next bar for the Steps tab of the Concept Model app, so a team working through the ten steps can move to the step before or after without reaching for the rail.

## The Problem

The Steps tab listed the ten steps in a rail down the left, and the only way to move was to click a row in that rail. A reviewer who worked through the app asked for back and next buttons to move between the steps. The page they were reading had no way on to the next step. On a shared screen, with a Facilitator talking the room through the steps, the eye is on the step, not on the rail.

## The Solution

A bar now sits fixed at the bottom of every step. On the left, Back names the step before, for example "Back: Step 1 - Identify the Scope". On the right, Next names the step after, for example "Next: Step 3 - Capture Business Stories". The bar stays in view while a long step scrolls, and it sits under the Map in Step 8 too.

## How It Works

Step 1 has no Back and Step 10 has no Next. The Parked list is not a step, so it has no bar. The bar moves, it never locks: every row in the rail is still one click away, so the steps stay a checklist the team can loop through, the way the Blue Book asks. A step now opens at its top, whether it was picked on the rail or with Back and Next.

## Key Benefits

- **The next step is where you are looking**: Back and Next sit under the step, named, so nobody hunts for the rail
- **Still a checklist, never a wizard**: no step is locked, and the rail still jumps anywhere
- **Every step starts at the top**: moving to a step never lands you halfway down it

The Back and Next bar is available now in the Concept Model app in the Context Plane monorepo, and it reaches the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model) and its [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/concept-model/) on its next publish.
