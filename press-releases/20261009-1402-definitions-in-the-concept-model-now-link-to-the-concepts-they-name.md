# Definitions in the Concept Model Now Link to the Concepts They Name

**9 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today made every Concept named inside a Definition a link in the Concept Model app, so a reader can follow a Definition to the Concepts it depends on in one click.

## The Problem

Good Definitions lean on each other. "A Customer is a person or organisation that has placed at least one Sales Order with us, through any Channel" only makes sense once you know what a Sales Order and a Channel are. On the Definitions tab those names were plain text, so a reader had to scroll and hunt for each one, and in Step 5 there was no way to see which other Concepts a Definition relied on.

## The Solution

On the Definitions tab, the first mention of another Concept in each part of a Definition is now a link, and a click brings that Concept's Definition into view. In Step 5, a **Refers to** line under the three parts lists every Concept the Definition names, and a click opens that Concept for defining.

## How It Works

The app finds a Concept by its name or any of its aliases, in any case, and as a simple plural, so "Sales Orders" and "orders" both find Sales Order when Order is one of its aliases. It matches whole words only and prefers the longest name, so "Sales Order Line" is not mistaken for "Sales Order". A Definition never links to its own Concept.

## Key Benefits

- **Follow a Definition in one click**: jump straight to the Concepts it depends on.
- **See the dependencies while defining**: Step 5 shows which Concepts a Definition leans on.
- **No extra typing**: the links come from the names and aliases already in the Model.

Linked Definitions are available now in the Concept Model app.
