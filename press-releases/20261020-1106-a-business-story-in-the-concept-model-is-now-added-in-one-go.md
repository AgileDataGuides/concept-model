# A Business Story in the Concept Model Is Now Added in One Go

**20 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released a blank new-story card in the Concept Model app, so a Facilitator captures a Business Story's title and the story itself in one step.

## The Problem

Step 3, Capture Business Stories, asks the expert to tell what happens, and the Facilitator writes it down live on a shared screen. Adding a story took two actions. First the Facilitator typed a short title and pressed Enter. Then they found the new card in the list, clicked its empty story line, and typed the story. Who told it and its Stakeholders were more clicks on that same card. While the expert kept talking, the Facilitator was hunting for the card they had just made.

## The Solution

Step 3 now opens with a blank story card above the list. It holds every field a story has: the title, the kind (happy path or variation), who told it, its Stakeholders, and the story in the expert's words. The Facilitator fills it in and adds it once.

## How It Works

Type the title and press Enter, and the cursor moves to the story. Click Add story, or press Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) from anywhere on the card. The story joins the list with everything filled in, the card clears, and the cursor goes back to the title for the next story. A half-written story waits on the card: go back to Step 2 to add the person who told it, and the story is still there when you return. Add story stays greyed out until the story has a title. The first story still starts as the happy path and later ones as variations, and the Facilitator can change that on the card before adding.

## Key Benefits

- **One step per story**: the title and the story go in together, so nothing is half captured
- **The cursor follows the conversation**: Enter moves from the title to the story, and adding returns to the title
- **Everything on one card**: who told it and who it matters to are picked before the story is added
- **Saved stories stay as they were**: every field is still a single click to change later

The one-step story card is available now in the Concept Model app in the Context Plane monorepo, and it reaches the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model) and its [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/concept-model/) on its next publish.
