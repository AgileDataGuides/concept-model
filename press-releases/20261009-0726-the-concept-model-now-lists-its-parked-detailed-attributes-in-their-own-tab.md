# The Concept Model Now Lists Its Parked Detailed Attributes in Their Own Tab

**9 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released the Parked Details tab in the Concept Model app, so a modelling team sees every detailed attribute it has set aside for the DESIGN stage in one place.

## The Problem

In Step 4 a Subject Matter Expert often names information about a Concept, like "Customer email", rather than a Concept itself. The Blue Book says these wait for the DESIGN stage, and the app had a field to park them. But the field was called "Park an attribute", and what it parked went onto one mixed list with out of scope items and questions for a future Map. A team that wanted to hand its detailed attributes on to the DESIGN stage had to pick them out of that list by hand.

## The Solution

The Step 4 field is now called "Park detailed attributes", and each item it parks is a Detailed attribute. A new Parked Details tab, between Definitions and Event Matrix, shows only those, with where each came from, and takes new ones directly.

## Key Benefits

- **A clean handover to DESIGN**: the detailed attributes are one list, ready to take into the next stage
- **Clear words**: the field says what it is for, information about a Concept, not a Concept
- **One click away**: the tab sits beside Definitions, so the two lists the DESIGN stage needs are side by side

The Parked Details tab is available now in the Concept Model app in the Context Plane monorepo, and it reaches the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model) and its [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/concept-model/) on its next publish.
