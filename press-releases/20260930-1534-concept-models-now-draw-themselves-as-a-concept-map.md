# Concept Models Now Draw Themselves as a Concept Map

**30 September 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released the Map tab for the Concept Model app, which presents the whole Concept Model as one picture, the Concept Map, drawn the way the Blue Book draws it, so a team can check that the picture tells the story.

## The Problem

A Concept Model is meant to be read by people who were not in the room. Until now the app showed it as lists and a table: Concepts on one side, Relationships in a grid, Events and Domains below. Nobody could see at a glance which things sit together, which Events join which Concepts, or where the story starts. Teams drew the Map somewhere else, on a whiteboard or in a drawing tool, and the drawing and the Model drifted apart the moment either one changed.

## The Solution

The Map tab draws the Model the way the Blue Book does. Concepts are blue sticky notes pinned to the page, each name in a handwritten brush font. Domains are sheets of paper taped behind the notes they hold. Core Business Events are diamonds carrying their who-does-what sentence, and every Relationship is a gentle green curve carrying its verbs, one read from each end once both are in: "places" sits near Customer and "is placed by" near Sales Order. Hovering a curve reads its Relationship Rules as plain sentences. The same Map sits inside Step 9 - Draw the Map, so a Facilitator can arrange it live without leaving the steps.

## How It Works

Drag a note or a diamond to place it, or nudge it with the arrow keys. Drag a Domain by its name to move the whole sheet, notes and all. Positions are saved with the model, so each Map keeps its own arrangement even when two Maps share a Concept. A Domain's sheet follows its Concepts: nobody draws or sizes it. Anything not yet placed gets a starting spot grouped by its Domain, so a new Model is readable before anyone arranges it. Pan, zoom and fit keep a large Map on one screen. The handwritten font ships with the app, so the Map looks the same with no internet connection.

## Key Benefits

- **One picture of the Model**: things, happenings and Domains read at a glance, in the organisation's own words
- **The same picture as the book**: a reader moves between the Blue Book and the app without translating shapes or colours
- **Never out of step**: the Map is drawn from the Model, so it changes the moment the Model does
- **Arranged in the room**: drag to arrange with the expert watching, and the arrangement is saved
- **Words on every line**: each line reads both ways once both verbs are in, and each rule reads as a sentence on hover

The Concept Map is available now in the Concept Model app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model) and in the [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/concept-model/).
