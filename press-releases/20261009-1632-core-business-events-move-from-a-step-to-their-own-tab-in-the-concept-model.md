# Core Business Events Move From a Step to Their Own Tab in the Concept Model

**9 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released a Core Business Events tab in the Concept Model app, so a modelling team finds its Events beside the Business Event Matrix that shows them, and the steps run as a clean sequence of ten.

## The Problem

The Concept Model listed the Core Business Events as Step 8, between the Relationship Rules and the Map. But a team works on its Events in two places: it lists them, then it marks them against its Concepts on a grid. The list sat inside the steps and the grid sat on a tab called Event Matrix, so the team jumped between the step rail and the top tabs to do one piece of work. The tab names were also shorter than the names used elsewhere: the picture is the Concept Map pattern template, and the grid is a Business Event Matrix, the same as the Business Event Matrix app. The tabs said only Map and Event Matrix.

## The Solution

The Core Business Events now have their own tab, directly to the left of the grid. The tab keeps Step 8's question "When does something important happen?", its description, the quiet hint for an Event with nothing attached, and the Event list where a team adds Events, picks the Concepts each one involves, the Relationship it sits on, and the Concept it is too. The steps after it move up one: Draw the Map is now Step 8, Gather Business Questions Step 9, and Walk the Map Step 10. The Map tab is now called Concept Map, and the Event Matrix tab is now called Business Event Matrix.

## How It Works

The tabs now read Steps, Concept Map, Definitions, Parked Details, Core Business Events, Business Event Matrix and Instructions. A link copied before the change still works: a link to the old Step 8 opens the Core Business Events tab, and links to the Map and the Event Matrix open the renamed tabs. The Excel export names its grid sheet Business Event Matrix to match.

## Key Benefits

- **Events in one place**: list the Events and mark them on the grid from two tabs side by side, without going back to the step rail
- **Ten steps, in order**: the step rail is a straight run from the Scope to walking the Map
- **Full names**: Concept Map and Business Event Matrix are the names the pattern templates already use
- **Old links keep working**: a link a team shared before the change still opens the right place

The Core Business Events tab is available now in the Concept Model app in the Context Plane monorepo, and it reaches the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model) and its [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/concept-model/) on its next publish.
