# The Concept Model Becomes the Companion App for the Blue Book

**30 September 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released the Concept Model app as the companion app for the Blue Book, an Agile Data Guide to Modeling Business Concepts, so a team can run the book's eleven steps with a Subject Matter Expert and capture what each step produces as they go.

## The Problem

The Blue Book teaches Modeling Business Concepts as eleven steps: find the Scope, find the expert, capture their stories, pull out the Concepts, agree what each one means, and on to walking the finished Map. A Facilitator running a session had nowhere to put most of that. The app held Concepts, Relationships, Core Business Events and Domains, but not the Scope, the people in the room, the stories the Concepts came from, the Business Questions, or the record of walking the Map. So the stories stayed in a notebook, the reasons behind the Model were lost, and six months later nobody could show where a Concept had come from.

## The Solution

The app now opens on a Steps tab. The eleven steps run down the left, each with its question from the book and a status that reads straight off the Model: "3 stories, 1 happy path, 2 variations", "10 of 16 agreed, 2 flagged". Click any step and its panel opens beside the list. Step 1 holds the Scope and the Domains. Step 2 holds the people in the session and their roles: the Subject Matter Expert, the Facilitator, the Data Team, and the Stakeholders who bring the Business Questions. Step 3 holds the Business Stories in the expert's own words. Step 4 puts a story beside the Concept list, so every Concept remembers the story it came from. Step 10 gathers the Business Questions, and Step 11 walks each story and question across the Map and records where it gets stuck.

## How It Works

The steps are a checklist, never a wizard, because the book says real sessions loop: a story sparks a Concept, the Concept needs a Definition, the Definition surfaces a Relationship. Every step is reachable at any time. A step can be skipped on purpose, with a note on why, so it is dropped knowingly rather than by accident. Every add field keeps the cursor after Enter, so the Facilitator can type as fast as the expert talks on a shared screen. The Domain, Concept and Event lists can be searched and dragged into order. The book's rules of thumb, like ten to twenty Concepts, show as quiet hints and never block anything. Things that belong elsewhere, like "Customer name" or a question for a future Map, go on a Parked list with a note on where they came from.

## Key Benefits

- **The book's process, on one screen**: every step's output has a home, and every step shows how far it has got
- **Built for the room**: fast capture on a shared screen, so the expert watches their words become the Model
- **Evidence kept**: the stories, the questions and the walks stay with the Model, so every Concept can be traced back
- **Nothing dropped by accident**: skipped steps say why, and parked items say where they came from
- **Older models still open**: a model saved before the eleven steps loads and moves up to the new format
