# The Concept Map Now Reads Each Relationship as a Sentence

**5 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released Relationship Rules on the Concept Map in the Concept Model app, so each line reads as the Blue Book's sentence, the verb and then how many: Customer "places one or many" Sales Orders.

## The Problem

The Map wrote each Relationship's verbs on its line, the verb near one Concept and the inverse verb near the other. The Relationship Rules agreed in Step 7, how many on each side and whether there could be none, only showed when someone held the pointer over a line. A team reading the Map on a shared screen, or in an exported picture, saw "places" but not "one or many", so the rule the Subject Matter Expert had agreed was missing from the one page meant to tell the whole story. The Blue Book's own Step 7 figure writes the rule words under each verb.

## The Solution

Each verb on the Map now has its rule words on a line under it: one, zero or one, one or many, or zero or many. Read from either Concept, the nearest verb and its rule make the sentence: "Customer places one or many" Sales Orders, and "Sales Order is placed by one" Customer. A rule end that is not agreed yet leaves its verb on its own. The rule words belong to the Verbs layer, so the Step 7 figure is the Map with Domains, Concepts, Relationships and Verbs on, and Export SVG carries them in the same handwriting.

## Key Benefits

- **The whole rule on the page**: the verb and how many, read straight off the line, with no pointer needed
- **The book's Step 7 figure**: the Map draws the verb, then its rule, as the Blue Book does
- **In every picture**: Export SVG and every layer choice carry the rule words with their verbs
- **Gaps show**: a verb with no rule words under it is a rule still to agree in Step 7

Relationship Rules on the Concept Map are available now in the Concept Model app in the Context Plane monorepo, and they reach the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model) and its [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/concept-model/) on its next publish.
