# Step 4 of the Concept Model Now Says Where Concepts Sit

**20 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released a guide line at the top of Step 4 in the Concept Model app, so a team knows where to start and how Concepts relate to the Domains it named in Step 1.

## The Problem

Step 4, Identify the Concepts, opens with the Blue Book's one-line description: the nouns in the story that the organisation counts, manages or tracks. A reviewer who worked through the app found it tricky to know where to start on this step. It was not obvious how the step related back to the earlier ones, and they asked for a description at the top that says this is the next level down from Domains.

## The Solution

A guide line now sits under the book's description at the top of Step 4: Domains from Step 1 are the big areas of the organisation, and Concepts are the next level down, the things inside each Domain that the organisation counts, manages or tracks. It then says how to work: one story at a time, reading it, adding its nouns as Concepts, and putting each one in its Domain.

## How It Works

The book's own words stay exactly as they are. The guide line is the app's own text, a little darker than the book's description so it reads as help. Any step can carry one, and Step 4 is the first to have it.

## Key Benefits

- **Know where to start**: the top of Step 4 says what to do first
- **See the link to Step 1**: Concepts are named as the level below the Domains
- **The book stays the book**: the guide sits under the book's description, never in place of it

The Step 4 guide line is available now in the Concept Model app in the Context Plane monorepo, and it reaches the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model) and its [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/concept-model/) on its next publish.
