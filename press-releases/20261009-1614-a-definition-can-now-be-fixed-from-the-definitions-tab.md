# A Definition Can Now Be Fixed Straight from the Concept Model's Definitions Tab

**9 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today added the Details popup to the Concept Model's Definitions tab, so a Facilitator can fix a Definition the moment someone spots a problem with it.

## The Problem

The Definitions tab is where a team reads its Concept Model through, often on a shared screen with the Subject Matter Expert. That is exactly when someone says "that is not quite right, a Customer has to have ordered something". The Definitions tab was read only. To make the change, the Facilitator had to leave it, open the Steps tab, find Step 5, pick the Concept, edit it, then come back and find their place in the document again. The conversation stalled while they did.

## The Solution

Every Concept card on the Definitions tab now has a **Details** button beside its status, and so does every Core Business Event. It opens the same Details popup the Steps use: the name, aliases, the three parts of the Definition with the broader category helper, examples, special cases, the Domain, the stories and notes. Save, and the card changes in place. The reader never leaves the page.

## Key Benefits

- **Fix it while it is being read.** A correction lands in seconds, while the Subject Matter Expert is still in the room.
- **Keep your place.** The Definitions document stays where it was, so the read-through carries on from the same card.
- **One popup everywhere.** The Details popup is the same one Step 4, Step 5 and Step 8 open, so there is nothing new to learn.

The Details popup on the Definitions tab is available now in the Concept Model app in the Context Plane monorepo.
