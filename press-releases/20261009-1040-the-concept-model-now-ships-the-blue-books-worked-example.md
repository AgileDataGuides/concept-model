# The Concept Model Now Ships the Blue Book's Worked Example

**9 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today added the Blue Book's retail worked example to the Concept Model app, and made it the model the app opens on, so a reader can open the same Concept Model the book builds, step by step, and follow along.

## The Problem

The Blue Book, an Agile Data Guide to Modeling Business Concepts by Juha Korpela and Shane Gibson, teaches its eleven steps on one worked example: a retailer that sells products through a website and physical stores, modeled from order to delivery. The Concept Model app is the book's companion, but it only shipped the SaaS Revenue example. A reader who wanted to see the book's own Map in the app had to type all thirteen Concepts, every Relationship and every Event in by hand before they could compare a single step.

## The Solution

The app now ships a second example, the Blue Book Retail Concept Model, beside the SaaS one. It holds what each of the eleven steps produces in the book: the order-to-delivery Scope across four Domains, the Retail Operations Manager as the Subject Matter Expert, the order, restock, return and team lead stories, thirteen agreed Definitions, the verbs and Relationship Rules on every line, the five Core Business Events, the Business Questions, and the walks, including the ones that got stuck. The Map opens in the arrangement the book draws, Sales top left and Human Resources bottom right.

## How It Works

The app now opens on the Blue Book Retail Concept Model. The SaaS example is one click away in the model switcher. The public demo seeds both examples on a first visit, and the public repo carries the new example in its `data/` folder.

## Key Benefits

- **Read the book with the Map open**: every step's output for the worked example is one click away.
- **See a finished Concept Model in a second domain**: retail beside SaaS shows the pattern is not tied to one kind of organisation.
- **Learn from the stuck walks**: the Return story and the late Delivery questions show how a Map handles what it cannot answer.
- **Start from the book's example**: copy it and change it to run your own session.

The Blue Book Retail Concept Model is available now in the Concept Model app and at github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model.
