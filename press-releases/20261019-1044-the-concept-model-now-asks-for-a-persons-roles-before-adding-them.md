# The Concept Model Now Asks for a Person's Roles Before Adding Them

**19 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released a new way to name the people in a modeling session in the Concept Model app, so a person can arrive in Step 2 with their roles already set.

## The Problem

Step 2, Find a Subject Matter Expert, lists the people in the session and the roles they hold: Subject Matter Expert, Facilitator, Data Team and Stakeholder. To add a person, you typed a name and pressed Enter, and the person arrived with no roles. You then had to find their card and click each role. A reviewer who worked through the app asked to set everything before adding, rather than adding first and picking the roles after. On a shared screen at the start of a session, every person cost two separate actions.

## The Solution

The roles now sit on a line above the name field, under the label "Their roles, picked before you add them". Pick the roles, type the name and press Enter, and the person arrives with those roles. The roles then clear, so the next person starts with none, and the cursor stays in the name field so the Facilitator can keep going.

## How It Works

The roles sit above the field, so a person reading down the page meets them before the name. The book's rule still holds: a person can hold two of the three modeling roles at a pinch, never all three, so the third greys out once two are picked. Stakeholder sits outside that limit. Roles stay optional, and each person's card keeps the same role chips to change later.

## Key Benefits

- **Name and roles in one go**: pick the roles, type the name, press Enter, so roles can go in with the name
- **Seen first**: the roles sit above the name field, where the eye meets them before Enter
- **The book's limit holds**: the third modeling role greys out before anyone can pick it
- **Room to grow**: the roles wrap onto more lines if the list ever gets longer

Roles before adding are available now in the Concept Model app in the Context Plane monorepo, and they reach the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model) and its [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/concept-model/) on its next publish.
