# The Concept Model Now Opens on the Three Pages a Blue Book Reader Uses

**12 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released a shorter tab bar for the Concept Model app, so a person reading the Blue Book sees the three pages the book works through, with everything else one click away under Other.

## The Problem

The Concept Model app had seven tabs in a row: Steps, Concept Map, Definitions, Parked Details, Core Business Events, Business Event Matrix and Instructions. A person reading the Blue Book for the first time works through three of them: the ten Steps, the Concept Map and the Definitions. The other four sat in the same row with the same weight, so a new reader could not tell which ones mattered. The app is the companion to the book, so its first screen should point at the book's own path.

## The Solution

The tab bar now reads Steps, Concept Map, Definitions, Other and Instructions. Other opens a short menu of Parked Details, Core Business Events and Business Event Matrix. When one of those pages is open, the tab names it, for example "Other: Business Event Matrix", so the tab bar always says where you are.

## How It Works

Click Other to open the menu, and pick a page to open it. A click anywhere else, or Escape, closes the menu. Each page works as it did before. A link copied before the change still opens the same page, including a link to the old Step 8, which opens Core Business Events. The hints that point at these pages now say where to find them, for example "under Other, in Parked Details".

## Key Benefits

- **The book's path first**: a new reader sees the three pages the Blue Book works through, and nothing competes with them
- **Nothing lost**: Parked Details, Core Business Events and Business Event Matrix are one click away, unchanged
- **Always know where you are**: the Other tab names the page that is open
- **Old links keep working**: a link a team shared before the change still opens the right page

The new tab bar is available now in the Concept Model app in the Context Plane monorepo, and it reaches the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model) and its [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/concept-model/) on its next publish.
