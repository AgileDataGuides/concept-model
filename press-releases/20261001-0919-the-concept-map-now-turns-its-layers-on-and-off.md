# The Concept Map Now Turns Its Layers On and Off

**1 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released layers for the Concept Map in the Concept Model app, so one model draws the picture for each step of the Blue Book, from Concepts on their Domains to the full Map with its Events, and Export SVG saves only what is showing.

## The Problem

The Blue Book shows the Concept Map growing one step at a time. Step 4 has only the sticky notes on their Domain sheets. Step 6 adds the lines between them. Step 7 adds the words on each line. Step 8 adds the Core Business Events. The app always drew everything at once. To show a team the Map as it stood at an earlier step, or to make the book's figure for a step, a Facilitator had to export the full Map and rub out the lines and words in a drawing tool, then do it again every time the model changed.

## The Solution

Five switches now sit above the Map: Domains, Concepts, Relationships, Verbs and Events. Each one shows or hides one kind of thing on the Map. Turn off Relationships, Verbs and Events and the Map is the Step 4 picture: Concepts on their Domains. Turn on Relationships for the Step 6 picture, Verbs for Step 7, and Events for Step 8. Nothing moves when a layer goes, so the notes stay exactly where they were placed. Export SVG follows the switches, so the file holds only the layers that are on.

## How It Works

The switches are a way of looking at the Map, not a change to the model, so turning a layer off never needs a Save. The app remembers the choice in this browser, and the Map in Step 9, the Map tab and Export SVG all follow it. Each switch stands alone, so the verbs can show without their lines. Verbs also holds the rule sentences that appear when you point at a line, so with Verbs off no Relationship text is left on the Map or in the file. An Event's dashed line to a Concept or a Relationship shows only while that Concept or Relationship shows, so no line points at nothing. Every exported file has the same page size whichever layers are on, so the pictures for each step line up page for page.

## Key Benefits

- **One model, every step's picture**: draw the Map as it stands at Step 4, 6, 7 or 8 without redrawing anything
- **Nothing moves**: hiding a layer leaves every note, sheet and diamond where it was placed
- **The file holds what the screen shows**: Export SVG saves only the layers that are on, and nothing hidden comes along
- **Figures that line up**: every export is the same page, so the step-by-step pictures sit in a deck or a book without jumping about
- **No Save needed**: layers are a view, so the model stays as it was

Layers on the Concept Map are available now in the Concept Model app, in the Context Plane monorepo and in the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/concept-model) with its [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/concept-model/).
