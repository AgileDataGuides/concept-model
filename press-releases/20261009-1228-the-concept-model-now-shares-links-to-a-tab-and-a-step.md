# The Concept Model Now Shares Links That Open a Tab and a Step

**9 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released shareable links for the Concept Model, so a facilitator can send someone straight to the tab and step they need to look at.

## The Problem

A Concept Model workshop runs over days, and the facilitator often wants a colleague to look at one thing: the Relationships in Step 6, or the Event Matrix. The app always opened on the Steps tab, at whichever step that browser last used. So every message came with directions: "open the app, click Steps, then click Step 6 on the left". People landed in the wrong place and lost the thread.

## The Solution

The address bar now names the tab and step on screen, and it changes as you click. Copy it and send it. The person who opens the link lands on the same tab and the same step.

## How It Works

A link looks like `?tab=steps&step=relationships`. The tab is one of `steps`, `map`, `definitions`, `parked`, `matrix` or `instructions`. The step is the step's short name, such as `scope`, `concepts`, `events` or `walk`. A link to an unknown step opens the step that browser used last. The link names a place in the app, not a Model, so the receiver sees that place in whichever Model they have open. To show them the same thing, tell them which Model to pick in the model switcher.

## Key Benefits

- **One click to the right place.** A colleague opens the exact step under discussion, with no directions.
- **Links survive changes.** A link names a step by its short name, not its number, so it keeps working if the steps are renumbered.
- **No clutter in history.** Clicking between steps updates the link without filling the browser's Back button.

Shareable links are available now in the Concept Model app in the Context Plane monorepo.
