# The Concept Model Now Separates the Verbs From the Relationship Rules, on the Steps and on the Map

**6 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released a change to the Concept Model that puts each Relationship's verbs in Step 6 and its how-many rules in Step 7, and lets the Concept Map show or hide each one on its own.

## The Problem

The Blue Book asks two different questions about a Relationship. Step 6 asks which Concepts are connected and with what verb. Step 7 asks how many, and could there be none. The app mixed the two: the inverse verb could be typed in Step 7 as well as Step 6, so a facilitator working through the rules found themselves still naming verbs. On the Map, one switch drew the verbs and the rule words together. A facilitator could not draw the Step 6 picture, verbs with no rules, for a slide or a workshop wall.

## The Solution

Step 6 is now the one place to name a Relationship's verbs, in both directions. Step 7 shows those verbs as a sentence and asks only for the how-many. If a verb is missing in one direction, Step 7 says to add it in Step 6. The Map now has a Relationship Verbs switch and a separate Relationship Rules switch, beside the Relationship Lines switch for the lines themselves. Turn on Relationship Verbs to see the Step 6 picture. Turn on Relationship Rules as well to see the Step 7 picture, where each line reads "places / one or many". Turn on Relationship Rules alone to see only the rule words on each line.

## Key Benefits

- **Each step asks one question**: the facilitator names verbs in Step 6 and agrees rules in Step 7, the way the Blue Book runs the session.
- **A picture for each step**: the Map and Export SVG can show the verbs without the rules, so the figure matches the step the room is on.
- **Rules on their own**: a reviewer can switch off the verbs and check only the how-many on every line.

The change is available now in the Concept Model app in the Context Plane monorepo.
