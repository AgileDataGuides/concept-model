# A Concept's Definition Now Reads the Same Everywhere in the Concept Model

**9 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today aligned the three places a Concept's Definition appears in the Concept Model app, so Step 5, the Definitions tab and the Details popup show the same three parts with the same words.

## The Problem

A Definition in the Blue Book has three parts: the nature of the thing, a few real examples, and the special cases. Step 5 edited those three parts, but the Details popup could not show the examples, the special cases or the status at all. The popup had its own "Definition" box instead, the Aristotle helper ("a Customer is a [broader category] that [distinguishing feature]"), which Step 5 could only show as a read-only line and the Definitions tab did not show. The same word, Definition, meant two different things, and a modeller had to open two places to see one Concept's whole Definition.

## The Solution

Part one now holds the Aristotle helper under it, editable in Step 5 and in the popup. When part one is still empty and both halves of the helper are filled in, **Use this sentence** writes it for you: "A Customer is an owner of a library card that can borrow books from our library." The popup gains parts two and three and the status, so a Definition can be finished from any tab. Part two now asks for real ones by name, and part three asks what counts and what does not, the way fact-based modelling grounds a definition in real instances and clear edge cases.

## How It Works

All three places, and the Event Matrix hover card, take their labels from one list and show the parts in one order: part one, part two, part three, status. Where part one is empty, the Definitions tab and the hover card read the helper's sentence instead. Use this sentence never writes over a part one that already has text. The popup's new layout is opt-in, so the Business Event Matrix and the Business Glossary keep the popup they have today.

## Key Benefits

- **One Definition, one shape**: the same parts, labels and order in Step 5, on the Definitions tab and in the popup.
- **A first sentence for free**: fill in a broader category and what sets the Concept apart, and the app drafts part one.
- **Finish a Definition from any tab**: examples, special cases and status are editable in the popup.
- **Better prompts**: real examples by name, and special cases written as what counts and what does not.

The aligned Definition is available now in the Concept Model app.
