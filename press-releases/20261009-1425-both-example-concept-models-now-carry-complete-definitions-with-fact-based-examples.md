# Both Example Concept Models Now Carry Complete Definitions, With Fact-Based Examples

**9 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today filled in every Definition in the Concept Model app's two example Models, the Blue Book retail example and the SaaS revenue example, and added an example written as a fact to each Concept.

## The Problem

The example Models are where a new modeller learns what a good Definition looks like, but they taught it unevenly. Most Blue Book Concepts had no broader category or distinguishing feature, several Concepts had one example or none, and some had no special cases at all. The SaaS Model's part one read as fragments ("A person or organisation that…") rather than the "A Customer is…" sentence the method asks for. A reader had little to copy.

## The Solution

Every Concept in both Models now has all three parts: a part one sentence, the Aristotle helper (a broader category and what sets the Concept apart), at least two examples, and special cases that say what counts and what does not. Each Concept also has an example written as a fact, the way fact-based modelling writes one: "Fact: Customer 'Harbour Café' places Sales Order 'SO-1042'." Where it helps, the same thing appears twice, once in everyday words and once as a fact.

## How It Works

The fact examples use the Model's own Relationship verbs, so they read as the Concept Map does and the Concept names in them link to their own Definitions. The Blue Book's own part one wording is unchanged. The open questions flagged in the SaaS Model, such as whether a Billing Period is a cadence or one actual period, stay flagged for the expert to rule on.

## Key Benefits

- **A complete Definition to copy**: every Concept shows all three parts filled in.
- **Two ways to read each example**: everyday words for the business, and a fact for the data team.
- **Edge cases named up front**: special cases like "a van run with three stops is three Deliveries" settle arguments before they start.

The complete example Definitions are available now in the Concept Model app.
