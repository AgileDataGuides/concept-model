# Step 6 Now Reads Each Relationship Left, Centre and Right

**9 October 2026**

AgileDataGuides today tidied Step 6 of the Concept Model app so each Relationship names its middle Concept once and reads across the row in three parts.

## The Problem

Step 6 showed the other way of each Relationship as a full second sentence: "Customer has Customer Delivery Location" beside "Customer Delivery Location belongs to Customer". The Concept that ends the first half was written again straight away to start the second half, so every row carried a name the reader had just read. The second half was also smaller and grey, so the inverse verb looked less important than the verb.

## The Solution

Each row now reads in three parts. "Customer has" sits on the left, "Customer Delivery Location" in the centre, and "belongs to Customer" on the right. The middle Concept is named once, both Concept names on the ends use the same bold font, and both verbs use the same italic style. Both verbs are still click-to-edit. On a narrow screen the three parts stack one under another.

## Key Benefits

- **Less to read**: each row drops a repeated Concept name.
- **Both ways at a glance**: the Concepts line up down the centre, with the verb on its left and the inverse verb on its right.
- **Equal weight for both verbs**: the inverse verb is no longer smaller and grey.

The three-part Step 6 is available now in the Concept Model app.
